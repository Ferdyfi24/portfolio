Zone 04 · Method

How I
work

Built by ops, powered by AI. Three steps, the same on every tool.

01

Write the rule

When a process breaks, I write down the rule it must never break again, in business terms.

ruleNo PO number, no delivery order.
02

Build the smallest tool

I describe the behaviour, AI writes the code, and the tool lives where the team already works: Sheets, Excel, a phone.

toolField app: check-in only inside 300 m.
03

Prove it breaks

Every rule gets a test, and the test is first shown to fail with the rule removed. Then it ships.

testRemove the rule, the test must go red.

About

I come from the business side, not software engineering. My work sits where the warehouse, the stores and the numbers meet: where stock really is, whether a delivery can be proven, and whether a report can be trusted.

I build with AI. I own the process, the rules and the checks; AI writes most of the code. I would rather ship a small tool that enforces one rule than a big dashboard nobody trusts.
